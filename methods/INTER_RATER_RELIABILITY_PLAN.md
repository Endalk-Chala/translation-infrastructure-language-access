# Inter-Rater Reliability Plan

This document specifies how reviewer agreement will be assessed after both researchers complete the independent human evaluation.

## Timing

Reliability is calculated **after both reviewers finish independent scoring and before adjudication**.

Do not discuss discrepant ratings before the reliability calculation.

## Numeric ratings

Each reviewer assigns 1–5 ordinal scores for:

- semantic fidelity
- pragmatic fidelity
- institutional fidelity
- actionability

Agreement should be calculated separately by:

- language: Amharic vs. Afaan Oromo
- evaluation dimension

Primary candidate statistic:

- **weighted Cohen's kappa** for the 1–5 ordinal ratings

A complementary statistic may also be reported if useful for the final analysis:

- **intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC)** for the numeric score structure

The final choice and reporting conventions should be confirmed after the completed rating data are inspected.

## Error-code agreement

Qualitative error codes are multi-label categorical annotations. Agreement should be evaluated separately from the 1–5 ratings.

Possible summaries include:

- raw percent agreement by error code
- positive agreement for each error type
- coder-by-code contingency summaries

Because multiple error tags can apply to one output, error-code reliability should not be collapsed into the same statistic used for the ordinal ratings.

## Adjudication

After reliability is computed:

1. identify disagreements
2. review disputed cases jointly
3. record the adjudicated score and final error code(s)
4. preserve the original Reviewer 1 and Reviewer 2 ratings

The adjudicated dataset is used for the final comparative analysis, while the original independent ratings support reliability reporting.

## Reporting template

A final methods section can report:

> Two researchers independently evaluated each blinded output using four five-point ordinal dimensions: semantic fidelity, pragmatic fidelity, institutional fidelity, and actionability. Inter-rater agreement was calculated on the independent ratings prior to adjudication. Disagreements were then reviewed jointly, and adjudicated scores were recorded separately while preserving the original ratings.

Exact reliability coefficients will be added after coding is complete.
